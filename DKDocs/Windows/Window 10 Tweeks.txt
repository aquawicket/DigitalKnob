*** Virus & threat protection setings ***
Turn off "Real time protecion", click Dismiss
Turn off "Cloud-delivered protection", click Dismiss
Turn off "Automatic sample submission", click Dismiss


*** Recycle Bin ***
Set Custom size to 100
Select Don't move files..
Check Display delete...
Remove Recycle Bin from desktop under: Themes -> Desktop Icon Settings

*** Hard Drive ***
Goto C: Properties
Uncheck "Allow files on this drive...",  click ignore all


CONNECT TO THE INTERNET

*** Register Windows ***
copy MAS_1.4_AIO_CRC32_9A7B5B05 to desktop and run it
press 2 for HWID Activation
delete MAS_1.4_AIO_CRC32_9A7B5B05 from the desktop

DISCONNECT FROM INTERNET
   
*** Visual Effects ***
Choose "Adjust for best performance"

*** Performance Options / Advanced ***
choose Adjust for best performance of "Background services"

*** Virtual memory ***
Choose "No Paging File" and restart

*** Colors menu ***
Turn off transparency effects
Choose Dark mode

*** Additional Power Setings ***
Create a power plan and choose High Performance or Ultra if available
Change "turn off display" to 3 minutes for Battery, 15 minutes for plugged in

*** Change advances power settings ***
> Power buttons and lid
   > Lid close action
      > On battery: Sleep
      > Plugged in: Do Nothing
   > Power button action
      > On battery: Shutdown
      > Plugged in: Shutdown
> Processor power management
   > Maximum processor state:
      > On battery: 90%
      > Plugged in: 90%


*** User Accounts ***
Change use account controll settings -> set slider all the way down


*** Taskbar settings ***
Turn off "Replace COmand Prompt with Windows Powershell"
always show all icons in notification area

*** Bluetooth & other devices ***
Set bluetooth to off

*** Sound Control Panel ***
Goto Sounds Tab -> Change Sound Scheme to: No Sounds
Goto Communications Tab -> Select: Do Nothing

*** Printers and Scanners ***
uncheck "Let Windows manage my default printer"

*** Turn Windows features on or off ***
Uncheck everything except ".NET Framwork 4.7..." and click OK

*** Apps & features ***
Click Manage optional features, Uninstall anything that appears


*** Startup ***
Turn off "Windows Security notification icon"

CONNECT TO THE INTERNET

*** Install / Update all device drivers
Download and install Driver Booster Free, scan and install all drivers
Uninstall driver booster

DISSCONNECT FROM THE INTERNET AND RESTART

*** Change adapter settings ***
right-click on each adapter, click properties, uncheck everything except TCP/IPv4, click OK

*** Mobile hotspot ***
Turn off "Turn on remotley"

*** Device Manager ***
click view, show hidden devices
Uninstall all devices not connected to the system (greyed out)  -> (Select, delete, enter)
Disable all Microsoft Network Device. WAN Miniports, Microsoft Wi-Fi Direct Virtual Adapters, Etc
Disable all Software devices
Disable all Cameras
Disable all Bluetooth adapters
Disable all Microphones
Disable any Unknown devices

*** App & browser control ***
Set "Check apps and files" to Off, click Dismiss
Set "Smartscreen for Microsoft tore apps" to Off, click Dismiss

*** File Explorer -> Folder Options***
> General
   Uncheck "Show recently used files in..."
   Uncheck "Show frequently used folders..."
   Click "Clear"
> View
   Select "Show hidden files, folders, and drives"
   Uncheck "Hide empty drives"
   Uncheck "Hide Extensions for known file typees"
   Uncheck "Hide protected operating system files"
   click OK

*** Typing ***
Turn off "Autocorrect misspelled words"
Turn off "Add a period after I double-tap the spacebar"
> Suggestion and autocorrections
   Turn off "English" and any other items that appear

*** AutoPlay ***
Turn Off "Use AutoPlay for all media and devices"
Change "Removable drive" to Take no action
CHange "Memory card" to Take no action

*** Date & Time
Turn off "Set time automatically"
Turn off "Adjust for dayight saving..."


*** Delivery Optimization ***
Turn off "Allow downloads from other PCs"
> Advanced options
   Check all 4 boxes and move all 4 sliders to the lowest setting

*** For developers ***
select "Developer mode"
> File Explorer
   click apply
> Remote Desktop
   Uncheck "Change settings to all connections... ", click apply
> PowerShell
   click apply
